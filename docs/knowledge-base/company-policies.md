# Bridgeway Labs — Company Policies

> Fictional company used for the AI Bridge learning project. All names, numbers and rules below are made up.

## About This Handbook

This handbook describes how we work at Bridgeway Labs. It applies to every full-time and part-time employee, and, where stated, to contractors and interns. Bridgeway Labs is a software company headquartered in Lahore, Pakistan, with a second office in Karachi. We build BridgeDesk, a customer support platform for small and medium-sized businesses.

The People Team owns this handbook and reviews it every January. If a policy in this handbook conflicts with your employment contract, your contract takes priority. If something is unclear, ask your manager first, and then the People Team at people@bridgeway.example.

Policies in this handbook are effective from 1 January 2026 unless a section says otherwise. Older versions of the handbook are archived on the intranet and are no longer valid.

## Our Values

Bridgeway Labs has four company values that guide decisions when no specific rule exists.

**Customers first.** We build for the small business owner who answers support emails at midnight. Before shipping a feature, ask whether it saves that person time.

**Write it down.** Decisions, designs and meeting outcomes are written in our wiki. If it is not written down, it did not happen. This helps colleagues in other offices and in different working hours.

**Own the outcome.** If you start something, you are responsible for finishing it or handing it over clearly. Blaming other teams is not accepted; fixing the problem together is.

**Be kind and direct.** Give honest feedback early, in private, and with respect. Disagree openly in meetings, then support the final decision.

## Working Hours

Bridgeway Labs core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Pakistan Standard Time (PKT), Monday to Friday. During core hours every employee is expected to be reachable on chat and available for meetings. Outside core hours, employees may choose when to work, as long as they complete 40 hours per week, or the hours stated in their contract for part-time employees.

Team meetings must be scheduled inside core hours. Meetings outside core hours are only allowed for incidents, for meetings with customers in other time zones, or when every participant agrees in advance.

Employees who work in customer support follow a shift schedule instead of core hours. Support shifts are published two weeks in advance by the Support Lead, and shift swaps must be agreed in the support channel and approved by the Support Lead.

Overtime is not expected. If you regularly work more than 45 hours per week, tell your manager so the workload can be adjusted. Employees on the on-call rotation receive PKR 3,000 for each on-call day, whether or not an incident happens.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are "office days" for everyone and cannot be remote days, because most planning meetings, design reviews and team lunches happen on those days. The other remote day can be any of Monday, Wednesday or Friday.

New employees must work fully from the office during their first 30 days. This onboarding period helps new colleagues meet their team, set up their equipment with IT Support and learn how we work. After 30 days the normal remote work rules apply.

Remote work days do not need approval, but they must be marked in the team calendar by 18:00 the day before. When working remotely you must be reachable during core hours, have a stable internet connection, and join video meetings with your camera on unless there is a good reason not to.

Working from a public place such as a café is allowed, but you must not discuss confidential information where others can hear you, and you must lock your laptop whenever you step away. Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the company VPN.

Remote work from outside Pakistan requires written approval from the People Team at least 14 days in advance. Working abroad is limited to 20 working days per calendar year because of tax and legal rules. Some countries are not allowed at all; the People Team keeps the current list.

Managers may temporarily ask a team to be in the office more often, for example during an important launch, but must announce this at least one week in advance.

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Employees who join during the year receive leave in proportion to the remaining months; for example, someone joining on 1 July receives 11 days for that year.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; the rest expire on 31 December. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March of the next year, otherwise they also expire. Unused leave is not paid out, except when an employee leaves the company, in which case remaining days for the current year are paid out with the final salary.

Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days must be requested at least 3 weeks in advance through the HR portal. Shorter leave should be requested at least 3 working days in advance. Your manager approves or rejects requests within 2 working days. Managers may only reject a request for a clear business reason, such as a product launch or too many people from the same team being away at the same time.

During the last two weeks of December, the office is closed between 25 December and 1 January. These days are not deducted from annual leave. Employees on the on-call rotation during that period receive two extra days of leave in January.

## Public Holidays

Bridgeway Labs follows the official public holidays announced by the Government of Pakistan, including Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Independence Day (14 August) and Quaid-e-Azam Day (25 December). Because some holiday dates depend on the moon sighting, the People Team confirms exact dates in the company channel as soon as they are announced.

Employees who must work on a public holiday, for example support staff on shift, receive one additional day of leave for each holiday worked, to be used within 60 days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year. If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the same day by message or phone, and mark the day in the HR portal.

For sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days, a doctor's note must be uploaded to the HR portal within 5 days of returning to work. If you need more than 10 sick days in a year because of a longer illness, speak to the People Team; long-term sick leave is handled case by case and is confidential.

You may also use sick leave to care for a sick child, spouse or parent, up to 5 of your 10 days per year. Mental health days count as sick leave and do not require a reason.

## Parental Leave

Birth mothers receive 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Fathers and partners receive 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave, which can be taken in one block or in two blocks within the first 6 months after the birth. Parents who adopt a child under the age of 5 receive the same leave as birth parents.

After returning from parental leave, employees may work a reduced schedule of 30 hours per week for up to 3 months with full pay. Parental leave should be announced to the People Team at least 8 weeks before it starts, when possible.

## Equipment and Laptops

Every engineer receives a company MacBook Pro, replaced every 3 years. Non-engineering staff receive a MacBook Air, also replaced every 3 years. Every employee also receives a monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset for the office, and a one-time home office budget of PKR 40,000 for a chair or desk.

Company laptops must have disk encryption (FileVault) enabled and must not be lent to anyone outside the company, including family members. Only software from the company software catalog or approved by IT Support may be installed. Automatic operating system updates must stay switched on.

A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to IT Support within 24 hours, at it@bridgeway.example. IT Support will remotely lock and erase the device. Reporting quickly is more important than knowing all the details; nobody is punished for losing a laptop, but late reporting is a serious violation.

When an employee leaves Bridgeway Labs, all equipment must be returned on the last working day. Employees may buy their 3-year-old laptop at a reduced price when it is replaced.

## Expenses

Employees can claim work-related expenses up to PKR 25,000 per month without manager approval. Claims above PKR 25,000 need written approval from a manager before purchase. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days through the Expenses app; late claims are not reimbursed.

Typical allowed expenses include books and online courses related to your work, travel to customer meetings, taxis after 21:00 when working late, and meals during business travel up to PKR 4,000 per day. Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment and upgrades to business class are never reimbursed.

Every employee also has a yearly learning budget of PKR 100,000 for conferences, certifications and training, for example Microsoft Azure certification exams. The learning budget is separate from the monthly expense limit, but courses above PKR 50,000 need manager approval.

## Business Travel

Business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. Domestic flights are booked in economy class through the Travel desk at travel@bridgeway.example. Hotels are limited to PKR 25,000 per night in Lahore and Karachi and PKR 18,000 per night in other cities.

Employees travelling for work receive a daily allowance for meals, as described in the Expenses section. Travel time on weekends is compensated with leave: one day of leave for each weekend day spent travelling.

## Code of Conduct

Bridgeway Labs does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind, whether in the office, online, at company events or with customers. This includes comments about someone's gender, religion, ethnicity, age, disability or appearance.

Employees must not accept gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than PKR 10,000. Any gift above that value must be reported to the People Team. Conflicts of interest, such as working for a competitor or a family member's company that sells to Bridgeway Labs, must be declared in writing.

Concerns can be reported to your manager, to the People Team, or anonymously through the Speak Up form on the intranet. Reports are investigated confidentially, and retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is a serious violation.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-review, feedback from two colleagues chosen by the employee, and a conversation with the manager. Salary changes are decided once a year after the December review and take effect from 1 January.

Promotions can be proposed by a manager at either review. Each promotion is reviewed by a committee of three senior employees from different teams to keep decisions fair across the company.

## Leaving the Company

Employees who resign must give the notice period written in their contract, usually one month. During the notice period the employee hands over their work and documentation, returns equipment on the last day, and has an exit interview with the People Team. Access to company systems, including Azure and BridgeDesk, is removed at 18:00 on the last working day.
